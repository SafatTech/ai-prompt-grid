> **AIPromptGrid — ready-to-copy prompts for stunning AI photo styles.**

### Shorter variants (for hero / App Store / ads)

| Length | Line |
| --- | --- |
| Ultra-short | Browse styles. Copy the prompt. Create. |
| Hero | Explore AI prompts by style — copy, paste, and create. |

### Positioning statement

> AIPromptGrid is a prompt library for creators who want polished, trend-ready photo looks — every style ships with a proven prompt, model tags, and a clear before/after, so you know what you’ll get before you paste.

### Category we own

**Not:** “another PromptBase / generic AI prompt marketplace.”  
**Yes:** “a clear grid of photo-style prompts — browse by look, copy what works.”

**Public messaging rule:** Lead with universal creativity and style discovery. Do **not** lead with a single country, culture, or one viral trend in brand/hero copy. Specific eras or places belong only on individual style pages when relevant.

## 3. Primary wedge

### The wedge

**Curated photo-style prompts with proof** — browse by style/concept, see before/after, copy a prompt tagged for the model it was tested on.

### Why this wedge wins

1. **Clarity over dump.** Most libraries feel like endless text walls. A grid of styles with proof images converts better.
2. **Trends move fast.** Fresh curation beats a huge stale catalog.
3. **Prompts alone are copyable.** Our moat is presentation, freshness, before/after proof, and (later) in-app generation — not locking text behind a paywall in v1.
4. **Search + social loop.** People find a look they want, copy the prompt, share the result. AIPromptGrid is built for that loop.

### What we lead with (content)

- Photo transformation & likeness-preserving styles  
- Vintage film, cinematic, fashion, portrait, and other popular looks  
- Whatever is peaking on Instagram / TikTok *this week* (as catalog items — not brand slogans)  
- Always tagged by **which model** the prompt was tested on (Gemini, ChatGPT, Midjourney, Flux, etc.)

### What we deliberately are not

| Competitor pattern | Our stance |
| --- | --- |
| Generic megacatalog of every Midjourney string | Small, curated, trend-fresh library |
| Paid prompt dumps with no proof | Free prompts + mandatory before/after |
| “AI art” fantasy / anime megahub | Photo-transformation styles first |
| Generator-first product on day one | Library first; generator waitlist → v2 |
| Country-branded homepage | Global, inclusive messaging for everyone |

## 4. Audience

### Primary

- Creators and everyday users who upload a selfie / photo into Gemini, ChatGPT, or similar and want a **specific look** that already works
- Anyone browsing for inspiration by style or concept

### Secondary

- Designers / marketers who need a quick, on-trend photo style for social
- Users discovering us via a shared before/after or search

### Jobs to be done

1. “I saw that look online — give me the prompt that works.”  
2. “I want a clear style, not a random prompt dump.”  
3. “Which model should I paste this into?”  
4. “Save this style so I can reuse it later.”

## 5. Tone of voice

**Reference:** Section tone and structure from [proxima.art](https://proxima.art/) — confident platform voice, clean section labels, creativity-forward without hype spam.

### Personality (3 traits)

| Trait | Means | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Creative & open** | “Creativity without limits” energy; invite exploration | Chaotic meme tone or overclaiming |
| **Clear & direct** | Short labels, copy-first UX, plain instructions | Hype jargon, fake scarcity, emoji walls |
| **Polished & calm** | Gallery/platform confidence; sections that feel intentional | Cringe nostalgia bait or niche-only framing |

### Voice spectrum

```
Formal ●――――――――○―― Casual     → platform-professional, lightly warm
Simple ○――――――――●―― Complex    → plain language; model notes stay precise
Serious ●――――○―――――― Playful   → confident, not meme dump
Reserved ●――――○―――――― Expressive → expressive in visuals; calm in UI copy
```

### Section tone (Proxima-aligned)

Use clean, universal section language like Proxima’s Explore / Featured pattern:

| Section type | Tone / label style | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Aspirational, brief | “AI prompts for images & looks. Creativity without limits.” |
| Featured | Gallery-forward | “Featured styles” / “Explore all styles” |
| Browse | Style / concept discovery | “Explore AI prompts by style / concept” |
| Detail | Practical, precise | “Copy the prompt. Tested on Gemini.” |
| Coming soon | Honest, light | “Video prompts — coming soon. Leave your email.” |
| Auth | Simple | “Sign in” / “Register” |

### Do / Don’t

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| “Vintage film look — tested on Gemini.” | Lead hero with one country or one viral trend |
| “Copy the prompt. Keep identity locked.” | “Secret sauce prompt (paywall)” |
| “Explore prompts by style / concept.” | “UNLEASH YOUR AI POTENTIAL 🚀” |
| “Video prompts — coming soon. Leave your email.” | Blurred fake pages that look like a scam |
| Speak to a global audience | Make the brand feel for one country only |

### Tone by context

| Context | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage hero | Confident, brief, universal | “Explore AI photo styles. Copy prompts that work.” |
| Style detail | Practical, precise | “Paste into Gemini. Identity stays locked; clothing and scene change.” |
| Social / TikTok | Punchier, visual-first | “This look — before → after. Prompt in bio.” |
| Waitlist / coming soon | Honest, light | “In-app generator is next. Want early access?” |
| Errors / empty | Helpful, no blame | “No styles in this filter yet. Try Vintage film.” |
| Legal | Plain and clear | Standard, readable Terms / Privacy |

### Words we prefer / avoid

| Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| style, prompt, look, create, explore, featured, before/after | magic, revolutionary, disrupt, unlock, secret |
| copy, paste, tested on [model] | one-click masterpiece (until we have a generator) |
| vintage, cinematic, portrait, fashion, viral look | ethnic, exotic, oriental; country names in brand/hero copy |
| coming soon, waitlist | blurred premium, “members only” tease with no product |

## 6. Messaging pillars

| Pillar | Message | Proof in product |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Proof over dump** | Every style shows before → after | Required images on style pages |
| **Explore by style** | Browse looks like a gallery, not a text dump | Grid + style/concept categories |
| **Model-honest** | Prompts tagged by where they work | Model filters + tags |
| **Free to grow** | Library free; generator later | No paywall in v1; waitlists for v2 |
| **Fresh over huge** | New styles ship fast | Admin CMS + content cadence |
| **Global & inclusive** | Anyone can use any look | Universal brand copy; no country-led positioning |
