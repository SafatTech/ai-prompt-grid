# Legal & Trust Notes — AIPromptGrid v1

**Status:** Draft outline (Phase 0) — not formal legal advice  
**Product:** AIPromptGrid  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-12

---

## 1. Trust principles (product)

1. **Honest coming soon** — waitlists only; no blurred fake catalogs.  
2. **Free prompts mean free** — no bait-and-switch paywall on prompt text in v1.  
3. **Proof images are ours to show** — synthetic or owned likenesses.  
4. **Model honesty** — only claim tested models.  
5. **Clear policies** — Privacy + Terms linked in footer before public launch.

---

## 2. Example images (before / after)

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| Generate synthetic people for demos | Use random people’s photos without permission |
| Use your own photos with consent of anyone pictured | Imply a real person endorsed a look they didn’t |
| Label AI-generated proof where required by platform norms | Present scraped social media faces as “results” |

---

## 3. Prompts & user responsibility

- Users paste prompts into third-party AI tools; **those tools’ ToS apply**.  
- Site copy should state prompts are for personal/creative use and users must follow model provider rules.  
- No assistance in the product for non-consensual deepfakes, CSAM, or illegal content (moderation stance in Terms).

---

## 4. Accounts & waitlists

| Data | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Auth email / Google profile | Account, favorites |
| Waitlist email | Notify about video prompts or generator only |
| Favorites | Service feature |

Privacy Policy must cover: what we store, Supabase/Vercel processors, retention basics, how to request deletion (process can be email-based in v1).

## 6. IP stance (working)

- **Prompts you write:** site claims license to display/distribute; users may copy for generation use.  
- **UI, brand, photos we generate:** owned by the project.  
- **User uploads:** none in v1 (no community upload).
