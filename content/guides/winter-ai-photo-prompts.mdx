# Winter AI Photo Prompts for Boys, Girls and Couples

**Quick answer:** Upload a clear photo and paste one winter AI photo prompt from this page. Keep the line that protects the face. The looks below cover snowy valleys, foggy roads, chai and shawl scenes, cozy aesthetic portraits, couple edits, and two-photo merges. Every prompt is free to copy and paste on AI Prompt Grid, and works in Gemini and ChatGPT.

## What is the winter AI photo trend?

The winter AI photo trend turns a casual selfie into a snowy travel or cozy hill-station portrait: puffer jackets, pherans, shawls, steaming chai, and soft falling snow, with your face left alone. Gemini is a strong pick for these short, face-locked edits. ChatGPT also works when you want a longer wardrobe list.

## How to use these winter AI photo prompts

1. Open Gemini and attach one clear photo (or two photos for the merge section).
2. Copy a prompt below and paste it in the same message.
3. Keep the face-preservation lines. Change only the clothes, pose, or backdrop if you customize.
4. If snow looks fake or a face drifts, send a short follow-up in the same chat.

## Tips for realistic snow, skin tone and faces

- Ask for soft, realistic falling snow and cold-air breath only where it looks natural. Heavy snow overlays often look fake.
- Keep the exact skin tone line in every prompt. Bright snow can push models to lighten faces.
- Match the light: one shared daylight or one shared streetlight so people look photographed together.
- For merges, use similar crops and similar light in both source photos.
- If a face drifts, send a short fix: keep both faces exact, restore each face separately, and change nothing else.
