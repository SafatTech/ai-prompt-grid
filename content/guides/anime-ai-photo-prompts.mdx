# Anime Transformation AI Photo Prompts for Girls, Boys & Friends

**Quick answer:** Upload a clear photo to Gemini and paste one anime AI photo prompt from this page to turn your photo into anime while keeping your real face. The looks below mix girls, boys, friends, couples, and chibi stickers so you can pick a vibe fast. Every prompt is free to copy and paste, and works in Gemini and ChatGPT.

## How to Turn Your Photo into Anime with Gemini

1. Open Gemini and attach one clear photo (or two photos for the cherry-blossom couple merge).
2. Copy a prompt below and paste it in the same message.
3. Keep the face-preservation lines. Change only the clothes, pose, or backdrop if you customize.
4. If a face drifts into a generic anime face, send a short follow-up in the same chat asking Gemini to restore the original face, hairstyle, and skin tone.

## Tips to keep your real face in anime style

- Keep the face-preservation lines in every prompt. Anime models love to invent a generic face.
- Ask for your exact skin tone, hairstyle, glasses, beard, or head covering by name in a short follow-up if anything drifts.
- Prefer a sharp, front-facing photo with even light. Beauty filters and heavy occlusion make identity harder.
- Want anime for your pet instead? Try the **[anime pet photo prompt](/styles/anime-companion)**. Prefer cartoon or 3D? See **[cartoon animation pet](/styles/cartoon-animation-pet)** or **[cute 3D toon pet](/styles/cute-3d-toon-pet)**.

Upload a clear photo to Gemini or ChatGPT, paste one anime AI photo prompt from this page, and keep the lines that lock face shape, hairstyle, and skin tone.

### How do I turn my photo into an anime character?

Use the meadow, saree/lehenga, power-aura, or college prompts above. They ask for an original generic anime illustration style while keeping you recognizable.

### Is the anime AI photo prompt free?

### Can I use these in ChatGPT as well as Gemini?

Yes. Paste the same prompts in ChatGPT with your photo attached. ChatGPT often follows longer wardrobe lists well; Gemini is usually quicker for short face-locked edits.

### How do I make a couple anime photo by merging two pictures?
