## Tiranga colours, kites and balloons (respectful use)

A tiranga ai photo prompt should never turn the flag into clothing. Use solid saffron, white or green in dupattas, lapel ribbons, flower trays, kite colour panels, and balloon bunches. Kites and balloons carry colour panels, not a flag replica. That rule applies to every prompt on this page.

## Parade viewing, rangoli, office ethnic day and sunrise looks
