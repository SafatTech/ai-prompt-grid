# How to Keep Your Face the Same in Gemini and ChatGPT (Why AI Changes Your Face and How to Fix It)

**Quick answer:** Start with a sharp, well-lit, front-facing photo. Write your prompt as an edit ("edit this photo of me"), list only what should change, and add a face-lock line: keep my face, features, skin tone and expression exactly the same. Change three or four things at a time, and if your face drifts, send a fix-up prompt in the same chat instead of starting over. In Gemini, avoid the Flash-Lite model for photo edits. In ChatGPT, comment directly on the part of the image you want fixed.

## Why does Gemini change my face?

Gemini and ChatGPT don't paste your photo into a new scene. They redraw the whole image, using your photo as a reference, so small details can come out slightly different each time. That's why the result can look like a cousin rather than you. The usual triggers:

- **The prompt reads like "generate", not "edit".** "A woman in a red saree at a palace" invites a new person. "Edit this photo of me" tells the AI to work from your face.
- **The face is small, blurry, side-on or shadowed.** The AI fills in what it can't see.
- **Too many changes at once.** A new outfit, location, pose, lighting and art style in one prompt leaves little room for your face.
- **Beauty words.** "Flawless skin", "perfect", "glamorous" or "fair" invite the AI to smooth, slim or lighten you.
- **Big style jumps.** Anime, 3D or caricature styles reinterpret faces by design.
- **Many people in one image.** Google says Nano Banana 2 keeps the resemblance of up to five characters in a single workflow. Larger groups are more likely to drift or blend faces.
- **Long chats.** After many edits, small changes add up.
- **The fast "Lite" model.** Google's Gemini help page says the Nano Banana 2 Lite option (used when the model is set to Flash-Lite) isn't designed for multiple image references or multiple edits.

## Why does ChatGPT change my face when editing photos?

The reasons are mostly the same. OpenAI says ChatGPT Images 2.5 (launched September 8, 2026) is better at preserving the subjects in reference photos and at editing only what you asked for. Its own prompting guide still warns that repeated edits can change details you meant to keep, and advises restating those constraints each time.

## What Google and OpenAI recommend

Both companies' guidance points the same way.

- **Separate changes from constraints.** OpenAI's guide says to write "change only X" and then list what must stay the same, such as identity, pose and lighting. Google's Nano Banana prompting guide says an edit prompt should focus on what is changing and what is staying the same.
- **Give each photo a role.** When you upload more than one image, number them and say what each is for ("photo 1 is me, photo 2 is the outfit").
- **Make one change at a time.** OpenAI suggests requesting one change per turn and repeating the details to preserve. Google says Gemini remembers earlier images in the same conversation, so step-by-step edits work.
- **Use the newest tools.** In Gemini, Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can regenerate an image with Nano Banana Pro from the three-dot menu. In ChatGPT, Images 2.5 lets you place comments directly on the image for focused edits.

## Before you start: the photo matters most

- Face the camera, eyes open, in soft daylight. No sunglasses or heavy filters.
- Your face should fill a good part of the frame. Crop closer if needed.
- Use a recent photo of yourself, and only edit other people's photos with their permission.

## Face-lock prompts (use these from the start)

### 1. The face-lock line (add to any prompt)

Paste this at the end of any prompt on AI Prompt Grid.

```
Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, hairstyle and expression exactly the same as in the uploaded photo. Do not beautify, slim, age, de-age or lighten my skin.
```

### 2. Change only the outfit (best in ChatGPT)

```
Edit this photo of me. Change only my outfit to a deep green shalwar kameez with a matching dupatta over one shoulder. Do not change my face, facial features, skin tone, body shape, pose, hairstyle or identity in any way. Keep the background, camera angle, framing and lighting the same, and make the fabric fit my existing pose naturally.
```

### 3. Change only the background (Gemini)

```
Edit this photo of me. Replace only the background with a softly blurred evening street with warm string lights. Keep me exactly as I am: same face, skin tone, hair, clothes and pose. Match the lighting on me to the new background so I don't look pasted in.
```

[IMAGE: before/after, Prompt 3]

### 4. Change the style but stay recognizable

```
Turn this photo of me into a soft watercolor illustration. Keep me clearly recognizable: same face shape, eye shape, nose, lips, eyebrows, hairline and skin tone, with any moles, beard or glasses kept in place. Only the medium should change, not my features.
```

### 5. Couples and groups: lock each person

```
Edit this photo of us. There are three people: left, center and right. Keep each person's face, age, height and skin tone exactly as in the original, and treat each one as a separate person; do not blend or swap features between them. Change only the outfits to matching festive white kurtas and the background to a lit courtyard.
```

### 6. Two photos: give each one a role

```
I've uploaded two photos. Photo 1 is me: keep my face, features and skin tone exactly as they are. Photo 2 is the outfit reference only: copy the clothing, not the face or body of anyone in it. Dress me in that outfit, keeping my pose and background from photo 1.
```

## Fix-up prompts (use these when the face drifts)

Send these in the **same chat**, right after the result that went wrong.

### 7. "That doesn't look like me"

```
Keep the outfit, background, pose and lighting exactly as they are. Only fix my face so it matches the original uploaded photo: same eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, face shape and natural skin tone.
```

### 8. My skin was lightened

```
My skin tone has been lightened. Change it back to match the original photo exactly, on my face, neck and hands, and keep everything else in the image the same.
```

### 9. Too smooth, too young or too slim

```
Remove the beauty retouching. Bring back my natural skin texture, my real age and my original face and body shape from the uploaded photo. Keep the outfit and background unchanged.
```

### 10. Faces blended or swapped in a couple photo

```
The two faces have been mixed. The person on the left must match the person on the left in the original photo, and the person on the right must match the person on the right. Restore each face separately and keep everything else the same.
```

### 11. A distinctive feature disappeared

```
Put back the details from the original photo that are missing: my glasses, my beard, the mole on my left cheek and my nose pin. Keep everything else the same.
```

Swap in your own details, such as a hijab, bindi, freckles, a scar or a gap in your teeth.

### 12. The expression changed

```
Keep the image as it is, but restore my natural expression from the original photo: the same gentle closed-mouth smile and the same eyes. Don't add a wider smile or show teeth.
```

### 13. The chat has drifted too far: reset

Start a **new chat**, upload your original photo and the last result you liked, and paste:

```
Photo 1 is the original photo of me; use it as the reference for my face, features and skin tone. Photo 2 is the edit I like; keep its outfit, background and lighting. Combine them so the result looks like photo 2 but with my face exactly as in photo 1.
```

## Tips for each tool

### Gemini (Nano Banana 2)
- **Avoid Flash-Lite.** In the Gemini app or gemini.google.com, make sure the model isn't set to Flash-Lite, then choose *Create images* and attach your photo.
- **Three or four changes at a time.** Lock the face first, then add changes in follow-ups.
- **Five people or fewer per image.** For bigger families, edit in smaller groups.
- **Try Pro.** Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can use *Redo with Pro* from the three-dot menu if a face still isn't right.

### ChatGPT (Images 2.5)
- **Comment on the face.** Place a comment on your face in the image, such as "match my original eyes and nose", so only that area changes.
- **Restate the lock line** in every follow-up, as OpenAI's guide recommends.
- **Number your uploads** when you use more than one photo.

## Common mistakes

1. **Using "generate" wording** ("a man in a suit") instead of "edit this photo of me".
2. **Asking for perfection.** Words like "flawless" and "fair" change faces.
3. **Changing everything at once.** Outfit, place, pose and style in one go is a recipe for drift.
4. **Starting a new chat for every small fix.** Fix in the same chat; reset (Prompt 13) only when it's gone too far.
5. **Using a tiny or dark face photo.** No prompt can rescue features the AI can't see.
6. **Expecting 100% the same.** These tools redraw the image, so expect "clearly me", not pixel-identical.

## FAQ

### How do I keep my face the same in Gemini AI?
Upload a clear, front-facing photo, start with "edit this photo of me", list only what should change, and add the face-lock line (Prompt 1). Avoid the Flash-Lite model, and fix drift in the same chat with Prompt 7.

### Why does Gemini keep changing my face?
It redraws the image instead of pasting your photo in, so a small or blurry face, generate-style wording, beauty words, big style changes or too many edits at once can all push it away from your real features.

### Why does ChatGPT change my face when editing photos?
For the same reasons. Images 2.5 is designed to keep subjects more recognizable, but OpenAI notes repeated edits can still change details. Restate what must stay the same and use comments to fix one area.

### Is there a prompt that keeps my face 100% the same?
No prompt can guarantee it. A clear photo, an edit-style prompt and the face-lock line give you the best chance. For pixel-perfect needs, edit the photo in a regular photo editor instead.

### How many people can Gemini keep consistent?
Google says Nano Banana 2 can keep the resemblance of up to five characters in a single workflow. With more people, split the photo into smaller groups.

### Which is better at keeping faces: Gemini or ChatGPT?
It varies by photo. Try the same prompt in both and keep the result that looks most like you.
