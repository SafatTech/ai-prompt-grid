# Karwa Chauth AI Photo Prompts

Karwa Chauth portraits are easy to overbuild. A moon, a sieve, bridal jewelry, and red clothing can turn two real people into a greeting-card illustration. The prompts on this page keep a photograph: both faces stay recognizable, the moon is part of the scene rather than a sticker, and any text is left out so it cannot be spelled wrong.

Copy a prompt into ChatGPT or Gemini with one photo of the couple. AI Prompt Grid does not make the image. These drafts are not a substitute for photos you have checked. Two related guides sit next to this one. For the lamp-lit festival that follows, read [Diwali couple AI photo prompts](/guides/diwali-couple-ai-photo-editing-prompts). For a period portrait that is not a festival at all, the couple and family version is [80s AI photo prompts for couples](/guides/80s-ai-photo-prompt-couple-family).

## What this solves

The usual request is "Karwa Chauth photo of us." The model then adds a giant moon, a third person holding a thali, and skin that does not match either partner. A usable prompt says which partner is fasting, what they wear, where the moon sits, and that no extra relatives walk into the frame.

Catalog styles that already handle formal South Asian clothing and warm light are a good base if you would rather start from a tested recipe: [South Asian Fashion Editorial](/styles/south-asian-fashion-editorial), [Shadowed Saree Noir](/styles/shadowed-saree-noir), [Intimate Cinematic Portrait](/styles/intimate-cinematic-portrait), [Golden Bouquet Glow](/styles/golden-bouquet-glow), and [Crimson Bangle Reverie](/styles/crimson-bangle-reverie). Use those when you want the look without writing the ritual into the prompt. Use the prompts below when the moon and the bridal styling are the point.

## Photo checklist

- One photo of both people, faces clear, taken in the last year if you can.
- Note who should wear the heavier bridal styling so the prompt does not dress both people identically.
- Jewelry in the source photo should stay if it is meaningful. Say so, or the model will swap it.
- A balcony, courtyard, or window is easier to extend into a night scene than a fluorescent office.
- If you want a sieve or a thali, the hands need to be visible in the source. Hidden hands get redrawn badly.

You can photograph in daylight and ask for evening. Say that the faces keep their real color anyway.

## How to run it

1. Start a new chat in ChatGPT or Gemini.
2. Upload the couple photo before the prompt.
3. Paste a single prompt. Leave app style presets off.
4. Check the moon last. Check the two faces first, then the hands, then the drape.
5. Repair from the original photo if a face moves. Do not iterate on the broken result.

## What must stay the same

Both identities, features, skin tones, ages, and recognizable details stay. Do not slim, smooth, or merge faces. Do not add parents, siblings, or a priest unless they are already in the photo, and even then keep the same count. Clothing and the time of day can change. Do not render writing on the sieve, the wall, or the moon.

## Balcony and moon

```prompt
Using the uploaded couple photo, create a Karwa Chauth evening portrait on a balcony. Keep both faces, ages, skin tones, and features recognizable and distinct. Do not beautify or blend them, and do not add people. Dress one partner in a red bridal lehenga with gold embroidery and a dupatta that does not cover the face, and the other in a cream or gold kurta. A realistic moon in the night sky, small and far away, not a graphic pasted on the image. Warm practical light on their faces, a simple sieve held naturally if hands are visible, realistic skin, no text, no logos.
```

## Window light, less jewelry

```prompt
Edit the uploaded photo into a quieter Karwa Chauth portrait beside a window at night. Preserve both identities exactly, including age and skin tone. Do not smooth skin or change the number of people. Add red and gold festive clothing that follows their real posture, modest jewelry, and the moon visible through the window as a normal moon, not a giant disc. Room light stays warm and photographic. No extra relatives, no written blessings, no watermark.
```

## Close portrait, faces first

Use this when the source is already a tight crop and a full balcony would force the model to invent bodies.

```prompt
Keep the uploaded couple in a close portrait and restyle it for Karwa Chauth. Same faces, same crop, same ages. Do not reveal new bodies the photo does not show. Add bridal color in red and gold at the edges of the frame, a hint of dupatta fabric, and soft evening light. No moon sticker on the forehead, no text, no third face, no skin smoothing.
```

## Repair prompt

```prompt
Return to the original uploaded photo. Restore both real faces, skin tones, ages, and the exact number of people. Keep red and gold clothing and a distant moon only if they do not cover faces or distort hands. Remove all text and any extra people.
```

## Common failures

- The moon becomes a flat circle with a face or a caption. Ask for a distant photographic moon and no text.
- Both partners receive the same bridal makeup and the same jaw. Style each person in a separate sentence.
- A sieve melts into the fingers. Skip the sieve unless the source shows hands clearly.
- The dupatta covers the eyes. Say it stays off the face.
- Strangers appear behind the couple. Repeat that nobody is added.
