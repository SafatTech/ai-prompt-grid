# Karwa Chauth AI Couple Photo Prompts for Gemini & ChatGPT (Keep the Same Face)

**Quick answer:** Upload a clear photo of you both (or just you) to Gemini or ChatGPT. Paste one of the prompts below and keep the line that tells the AI to preserve your faces. Fix any drift with a short follow-up message rather than starting over. The prompts follow the real day in order: sargi before sunrise, mehendi and dressing up, the evening puja, the moon through the chalni, and the first sip of water that breaks the fast. There's also a prompt for when your husband is away and you only have separate photos.

[IMAGE: hero before/after. Left: a casual couple selfie. Right: the same couple on a terrace at moonrise, the wife looking at her husband through a decorated chalni with a diya glowing. Caption: "Your faces, your moonrise."]

## Why these prompts edit your photo instead of inventing one

Many Karwa Chauth prompt lists describe "an Indian couple on a terrace". Paste that with your photo and the AI may draw two strangers. Every prompt here starts with "edit this photo of us", then names what should change (outfits, setting, light) and what must stay the same (faces, skin tones, expressions).

## Before you start

- **Faces clear and well lit.** No sunglasses, no heavy shadows. A daytime photo is fine; the prompt adds the moonlight.
- **Faces reasonably large.** Crop closer if you're small in the frame.
- **Hands visible if you want mehendi.** The AI needs to see your hands to draw on them.
- **Only upload photos of people who agreed to it.**

## Karwa Chauth couple photo prompts

### 1. Looking through the chalni at moonrise (best in Gemini)

The classic shot, short enough for Gemini to follow every detail.

```
Edit this photo of us into a Karwa Chauth moonrise moment on a terrace. Keep both of our faces, skin tones and expressions exactly the same. Do not blend or swap features. She holds a decorated steel chalni (sieve) and looks at him through it, with a small lit diya on a pooja thali beside her. Red silk saree with a gold border and mehendi on her hands; he wears a cream kurta. Full moon in a deep blue sky, warm diya glow on our faces.
```

### 2. Breaking the fast: the first sip of water (best in ChatGPT)

ChatGPT handles a detailed, step-by-step scene well.

```
Using the uploaded photo of us, create the moment the Karwa Chauth fast is broken. Preserve both faces completely: same face shape, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone and natural skin texture for each person. Do not beautify, lighten or change either face.
Him: offering her a sip of water from a small decorated karwa (spouted pot), with a tender smile.
Her: red bridal-style lehenga, mehendi on both hands, red and gold bangles, sindoor, small bindi, holding a pooja thali with a lit diya, sweets and a chalni.
Setting: a rooftop at night, the full moon behind us, fairy lights on the railing.
Lighting: warm diya glow on our faces, cool moonlight from behind.
Framing: waist-up, 4:5, both faces sharp.
```

### 3. Waiting for the moon together

```
Turn this photo of us into a candid Karwa Chauth evening scene: the two of us on a rooftop, leaning on the railing and looking up at the sky, waiting for the moon. Preserve each person's real face, skin tone and hairstyle separately. Her in a maroon saree with the pallu over one shoulder, him in a navy kurta. Twilight sky, city lights below, a pooja thali with a chalni and diya resting on the ledge. Natural, unposed photo look.

She holds a pooja thali with a karwa, diya and chalni. Decorated terrace with marigold garlands and diyas, full moon rising, warm festive light.

### 5. Husband away? Combine two photos into one (Gemini)

Useful when your husband is travelling or living abroad. Upload **both** photos in one message.

```
I've uploaded two photos: photo 1 is the wife, photo 2 is the husband. Create one Karwa Chauth moonrise photo of them together on a terrace: she looks at him through a decorated chalni, holding a pooja thali with a lit diya. Keep the wife's face and skin tone exactly as in photo 1 and the husband's exactly as in photo 2; do not mix their features. Festive red outfit for her, kurta for him, full moon behind them, the same warm lighting on both so they look photographed together.
```

**Tip:** Photos with similar light and head angles blend most naturally.

## Sargi and family prompts

### 10. Sargi at dawn

The pre-dawn sargi meal is traditionally sent or prepared by the mother-in-law.

```
Edit this photo into a Karwa Chauth sargi scene before sunrise. Keep every person's face, age and skin tone exactly the same; do not blend features. Sitting at a dining table with a sargi thali of fresh fruit, dry fruits, pheniyan, mathri and sweets. Soft lamp light with a hint of blue dawn through the window, cozy and quiet mood.
```

**Tip:** For a mother-in-law and daughter-in-law photo, upload a photo of you both and add: *"The older woman is handing the sargi thali to the younger woman, both smiling."*

### 11. Evening puja with friends

The evening katha often happens in a circle, with women passing their thalis around.

```
Edit this group photo into a Karwa Chauth evening puja gathering. Keep every woman's face, age and skin tone exactly the same; treat each person as a separate identity. Everyone in red, maroon and pink festive outfits with mehendi, sitting in a circle on a floor rug, each holding a decorated thali with a diya and karwa. Keep the center of the circle simple, with flowers and diyas only; no idols or deity images. Warm festive light, happy candid expressions.

## Keep-the-same-face fix

### 13. Repair faces, skin tone or mehendi hands

Send this in the **same chat** if a face changed or the hands look odd.

```
Keep the outfits, background and lighting exactly as they are. Only fix the faces so each person matches the original uploaded photo: same eyes, nose, lips, jawline and natural skin tone. ## Tips for each tool

### Gemini (Nano Banana 2)
- **Find it:** In the Gemini app or gemini.google.com, choose *Create images*, attach your photo, and paste the prompt.
- **Three or four changes at a time.** Outfits and setting first, then moon and diya light in a follow-up.
- **Fix in the same chat.** "Only fix her hands" beats starting over. Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can also use *Redo with Pro* from the three-dot menu.

### ChatGPT (Images 2.5)
- **Go detailed.** Separate lines for each person's outfit and jewellery work well (see Prompt 2).
- **Comment on one spot.** Images 2.5 lets you place a comment on part of the image, such as "make the chalni round and metal", to fix just that area. OpenAI says it also keeps earlier changes more consistent across several edits.

## Common mistakes

1. **Using a "generate" prompt.** If it says "a couple" instead of "us", you may get strangers. Start with "edit this photo of us".
2. **Skipping the skin-tone line.** Warm diya light can push the AI to lighten or darken skin. Ask for your *natural* skin tone.
3. **Getting the ritual order mixed up.** The moon is seen through the chalni first, then the husband through the same chalni, then he offers water. A thali with a karwa, diya and chalni makes the scene read correctly.
4. **Asking for deities in the frame.** AI can render sacred images badly. Keep puja scenes to the thali, diyas, flowers and karwa.
5. **Expecting perfect hands.** Mehendi and bangles are fine detail, and hands are where AI slips most. Use Prompt 13 or regenerate.
6. **Leaving it to Oct 29.** Free plans have usage limits, and many people are editing on the day. Make your images a few days early.

## FAQ

### How do I make a Karwa Chauth couple photo in Gemini?
Choose *Create images* in Gemini, upload a clear photo of you both, and paste a couple prompt such as Prompt 1. Keep the line about preserving both faces.

### Can I make a Karwa Chauth photo if my husband is away?
Yes. Upload a photo of each of you in the same message, say which photo is which person, and ask for matching lighting (Prompt 5).

### How do I keep my face the same in Karwa Chauth AI photos?
Say "edit this photo", list only what should change, and add "keep my face and skin tone exactly the same". If something still drifts, use the fix-up prompt (Prompt 13) in the same chat.

### Can AI add mehendi to my hands?
Yes. Ask for "fine bridal mehendi on both hands" and make sure your hands are visible in the original photo. Detail can vary between runs, so generate a few and keep the best.

### Gemini or ChatGPT: which is better for Karwa Chauth photos?
Both work. Gemini is often strong at keeping faces; ChatGPT handles long outfit and jewellery descriptions well. Try your favorite prompt in both.

### Are these Karwa Chauth prompts free?
Yes, the prompts are free to copy and paste. Gemini and ChatGPT both offer image editing on free plans with usage limits.
