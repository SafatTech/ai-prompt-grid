# LinkedIn Profile Picture Prompts for Gemini: Headshot & CV Photo Prompts for Men and Women

**Quick answer:** Upload a clear, front-facing photo to Gemini or ChatGPT and paste one of the prompts below. Each one changes your clothes, background and lighting while telling the AI to keep your face, skin tone and features exactly the same, because a profile photo should still look like you. There are looks for men and women, office and plain-background CV versions, and hijab- and dupatta-friendly options. **Don't use any of these for a passport, CNIC or other ID photo** (see the next section).

[IMAGE: hero before/after. Left: a casual phone selfie. Right: the same person in a navy blazer against a soft grey background, face unchanged.]

## Important: not for passports, CNIC or ID cards

AI-edited photos are for LinkedIn, CVs, company websites and email signatures. **Do not use them on a passport, CNIC, visa or any other official ID.**

- Pakistan's passport office (DGIP) says photos must be professionally taken on a plain white background and must not be edited in software such as Photoshop.
- NADRA's Pak ID app has you capture your CNIC photo live in the app.
- The US State Department says plainly: do not change your passport photo with filters or artificial intelligence, and it checks photos for AI use.

Other countries' rules are similar. An AI-edited ID photo can get your application rejected, and false information on a passport application can carry penalties. For ID photos, visit a photo studio or follow your issuing office's own instructions.

## Why "edit this photo" prompts work better

A prompt like "a professional man in a suit" makes a stranger. LinkedIn says your profile photo must reflect your likeness. It allows AI to enhance or edit a picture, but the result should still be you. So every prompt here starts with "edit my photo", lists what should change, and says what must stay the same.

## Before you start

- **Use a recent photo.** People should recognize you when they meet you.
- **Face the camera, in even light.** Daylight from a window works well. No sunglasses, no heavy shadows.
- **Head and shoulders in frame.** Crop out other people first.
- **Keep it honest.** Change the outfit and background, not your face, age or skin tone.

## LinkedIn headshots

### 1. Classic LinkedIn headshot (best in Gemini)

```
Edit my photo into a professional LinkedIn headshot. Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, hairstyle and expression exactly the same; do not beautify, slim or lighten my skin. Change my clothes to a well-fitted navy blazer over a plain white shirt. Use a soft, evenly lit light-grey studio background, flattering soft light, head-and-shoulders framing, sharp focus on my eyes, square crop.
```

### 2. Coat pant / suit headshot for men (best in ChatGPT)

ChatGPT handles a detailed list well.

```
Using my uploaded photo, create a corporate headshot. Preserve my face completely: same face shape, eyes, nose, lips, beard or moustache, hairline, skin tone and natural skin texture. Do not make me look younger or change my skin.
Outfit: charcoal grey two-piece suit (coat pant), light blue shirt, dark tie.
Background: soft out-of-focus neutral grey.
Lighting: soft studio light from the front left, no harsh shadows.
Framing: head and shoulders, slight angle, square, eyes sharp.
```

### 3. LinkedIn headshot for women

```
Edit my photo into a professional LinkedIn headshot. Keep my face, skin tone, hairstyle and expression exactly the same. Change my outfit to a tailored black blazer over a cream high-neck top. Soft beige studio background, gentle even light, light natural makeup only, head-and-shoulders framing, square crop.
```

## CV and office photos

### 7. Simple CV photo

For CVs and job portals that ask for a photo. **This is not an ID photo.**

```
Edit my photo into a clean CV photo. Keep my face, features, skin tone and hairstyle exactly the same. Plain light background, smart formal clothing, soft even lighting with no shadows on the face, facing the camera with a slight friendly smile, head and upper shoulders, sharp focus.
```

### 8. Office background headshot

```
Edit my photo so I'm in a modern office. Keep my face, skin tone and expression exactly the same. Smart business clothing. Behind me: a softly blurred bright office with glass walls and plants, natural window light on my face, head and shoulders, shallow depth of field.
```

[IMAGE: before/after, Prompt 8]

### 9. Outdoor natural-light headshot

A friendlier look for freelancers, teachers and creatives.

```
Edit my photo into an approachable outdoor headshot. Keep my face, skin tone and hairstyle exactly the same. Smart-casual outfit (a plain shirt or kurta in a solid color). Softly blurred green trees behind me, late-afternoon light, natural warm smile, head and shoulders.
```

### 10. Clean-up only (most natural)

When you like your photo and just want it tidier.

```
Edit my photo with minimal changes. Keep my face, features, skin tone and clothes exactly the same. Only replace the background with a soft plain grey, even out the lighting, and remove small stray hairs and lint. No skin smoothing, slimming or makeup changes.
```

## For teams and banners

### 11. Matching team headshots

Run this once per person, in separate chats, with the same wording, so a team page looks consistent.

```
Edit this person's photo into a company team headshot. Keep their face, features, skin tone and hairstyle exactly the same. Soft light-grey studio background, even front lighting, head-and-shoulders framing with the eyes about one-third from the top, square crop. Keep their own outfit but neaten the collar.
```

### 12. LinkedIn cover banner

No face needed. LinkedIn recommends 1584 × 396 pixels for the background photo.

```
Create a wide LinkedIn background banner, 4:1. A clean, modern abstract design in navy and soft teal with subtle geometric lines, lots of empty space on the left for my profile photo, no text, no people, no logos.
```

## Keep-the-same-face fix

### 13. "It doesn't look like me"

Send this in the **same chat**.

```
Keep the clothes, background and lighting exactly as they are. Only fix my face so it matches my original photo: same eyes, nose, lips, jawline, age and natural skin tone. Remove any skin smoothing or lightening.
```

## Tips for each tool

### Gemini (Nano Banana 2)
- **Find it:** In the Gemini app or gemini.google.com, choose *Create images*, attach your photo and paste the prompt.
- **Three or four changes at a time.** Outfit and background first, lighting in a follow-up.
- **Fix in the same chat.** Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can also try *Redo with Pro* from the three-dot menu.

### ChatGPT (Images 2.5)
- **Comment on one spot.** You can comment on part of the image, such as "straighten the collar", to fix just that area.
- **Small steps.** OpenAI says Images 2.5 keeps earlier changes more consistent across several edits.

## Common mistakes

1. **Over-smoothing.** Plastic skin looks fake at once. Ask for "natural skin texture".
2. **Lightening skin.** Always include "keep my skin tone exactly the same".
3. **Changing your face or age.** A headshot that looks ten years younger confuses people in interviews.
4. **Busy backgrounds.** Keep them soft and plain so your face stands out.
5. **Removing your hijab, glasses or beard.** If you wear them day to day, tell the AI to keep them.
6. **Using it as an ID photo.** Passports and CNICs need unedited photos taken under official rules.

## FAQ

### What is the best LinkedIn profile picture prompt for Gemini?
Start with Prompt 1: it changes the outfit and background while keeping your face, skin tone and expression the same. Women can use Prompt 3 or 4, and men in suits can use Prompt 2.

### Can I use Gemini without changing my face?
Usually, if your photo is clear and you say "keep my face exactly the same; do not beautify". If it drifts, use Prompt 13 in the same chat.

### Is an AI headshot allowed on LinkedIn?
LinkedIn says your profile photo must reflect your likeness. It allows AI to enhance or edit pictures, but you shouldn't use someone else's face or a made-up person.

### Can I make a passport-size or CNIC photo with Gemini?
No. Pakistan's passport office says not to edit passport photos in software, NADRA takes CNIC photos live in its app, and the US State Department says not to use AI. Use a photo studio or your issuing office's process.

### What size should my LinkedIn photo be?
LinkedIn accepts PNG or JPG profile photos between 400 × 400 and 7680 × 4320 pixels, up to 8MB. Square crops work best.

### Does this work in ChatGPT too?
Yes. All the prompts work in ChatGPT. Prompt 2 is written in ChatGPT's preferred detailed style.
