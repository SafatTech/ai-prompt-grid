# Halloween AI Prompts for Selfies

Halloween prompts go wrong when they hide the person you uploaded. A mask, a full skull overlay, or "make me a vampire" often replaces the face instead of dressing it. The prompts below keep the face uncovered and recognizable, then add costume, light, and a room that looks like a party rather than a movie poster.

You copy the prompt into ChatGPT or Gemini and upload one selfie. AI Prompt Grid does not generate the photo. These are drafts: run them on a real selfie and replace the placeholders before you publish a result. If the Halloween photo is of a pet, use [AI pet Halloween costume prompts](/guides/ai-pet-halloween-costume-prompts) instead of forcing a person prompt onto an animal.

## What this solves

A costume edit should answer four questions: what is on the body, what stays on the face, where the person is standing, and what the camera flash is doing. "Spooky" does not answer any of them. The model fills the gap with fog, extra fingers, and a face that belongs to someone else.

These prompts are for one person in frame, or a couple already standing together. They are not for building a group out of separate selfies. Catalog styles that already aim at dramatic light, rather than a costume, are [Crimson Bloom Shadows](/styles/crimson-bloom-shadows), [Velvet Rose Noir](/styles/velvet-rose-noir), and [Silver Rim Noir](/styles/silver-rim-noir). [Vintage Pulp Comic Hero](/styles/vintage-pulp-comic-hero) is the closer match if you want an illustrated character instead of a photographed costume.

## Photo checklist

- Use a recent selfie with both eyes visible and the face larger than a thumbnail.
- Take off sunglasses. A costume hat is easier to add than a face the model cannot see.
- If you wear glasses every day, keep them in the prompt so the result does not erase them.
- Prefer a simple background. A busy street becomes a second scene the model has to invent.
- For a couple costume, start from one photo of both people. Do not upload two solo selfies and ask for a shared scene.

Front light is enough. You do not need a studio. A window or a porch light is a better source than a photo that is already a Halloween filter.

## How to run it

1. Start a fresh chat in ChatGPT or Gemini so an older costume does not carry over.
2. Upload the selfie, then paste one prompt.
3. Generate a single image. Turn off extra style buttons if the app offers them.
4. Compare the result with the selfie at the eyes, nose, and jaw before you look at the costume.
5. If the face slipped, rerun from the original selfie with the repair prompt. Do not keep stacking "spookier" on a bad face.

The upload path is the same one described in [how it works](/how-it-works). For a tested look that is not a costume, start from [Explore](/explore).

## What must stay the same

The face stays uncovered: no mask, no skull paint that erases features, no veil across the eyes. Identity, age, skin tone, and recognizable features stay. Do not slim or smooth the face. Hair can be restyled around the costume. The number of people stays the same as the upload.

## Witch portrait, face uncovered

```prompt
Using my uploaded selfie, dress me as a Halloween witch while keeping my face fully visible and recognizable. Preserve my identity, facial features, skin tone, age, and glasses if I am wearing them. Do not slim, smooth, or beautify my face, and do not cover it with a mask. Add a black pointed hat set back off the forehead, a wool coat, and a small broom held naturally in one hand. Background is a porch at dusk with one warm bulb and a few paper bats, not a fog-filled castle. Direct flash on the face, slight film grain, realistic skin texture. No extra people, no readable text, no logos.
```

## Couple costume from one photo

Only use this when both people are already in the picture.

```prompt
Using the uploaded photo of both of us, create a Halloween party portrait. Keep both faces uncovered and recognizable, with the same ages, skin tones, and features. Do not blend our faces or add anyone else. Dress one of us in a simple vampire costume with a dark collar and a hint of fang that does not hide the mouth, and the other in a black cat-ear headband with a satin dress. Indoor party lighting, orange paper lanterns, a bowl of candy on a table behind us, direct flash, and natural skin. No masks, no extra limbs, no readable brand names, no text.
```

## 1980s basement party

This version is a photographed costume, not a cinematic poster. The face stays yours.

```prompt
Turn my uploaded selfie into a guest at a homemade 1980s basement Halloween party. Preserve my identity, face, skin tone, and age. Keep my face fully uncovered, with no mask and no beautifying. Costume is a cardboard-and-foil robot or a simple sheet ghost with the face opening large enough that my real face is clear. Rec-room paneling, paper streamers, a card table, direct flash, faded color, and film grain. No modern phones. No additional people. No text except a small date stamp reading 10 31 86.
```

## Repair prompt

```prompt
Start again from the original selfie, not from the last image. Restore my real face, age, skin tone, and features, and keep the face uncovered. You may keep the Halloween costume, hat, and party background. Do not add people, masks, or plastic skin. Hands must look natural.
```

## Common failures

- The hat shadow eats the eyes. Say the hat sits back and the face is evenly flashed.
- Fangs or face paint replace the mouth. Ask for a hint of costume, not a new face.
- A second person walks in from the edge. Repeat the headcount.
- The background becomes a fantasy castle. Name an ordinary room.
- Hands melt around a broom or a cup. Ask for natural hands and check them.

## Pets, and what this page is not

If the subject is a dog or cat, do not use these selfie prompts. The face rules are different, and a costume that covers a muzzle changes the animal people recognize. The paired guide is [AI pet Halloween costume prompts](/guides/ai-pet-halloween-costume-prompts).

These prompts are unpublished drafts until the placeholders are real photos and the lines have been tried on more than one selfie. The edit happens in your external editor, not on this site.
