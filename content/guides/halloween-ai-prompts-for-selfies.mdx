# Halloween AI Prompts for Selfies in Gemini & ChatGPT: Ghostface Y2K, Costumes & More

**Quick answer:** Upload a clear, well-lit selfie to Gemini or ChatGPT and paste one of the prompts below. Keep the line that tells the AI to preserve your face. If something drifts, fix it with a short follow-up message rather than starting over. For this year's most-shared look, the **Ghostface Y2K** scene, start with Prompt 1. For classic costumes that still look like you, jump to Prompts 5–10. There's also a short pet section at the end.

## What's trending this Halloween

The biggest Halloween AI look of 2026 is **nostalgic horror**, not gore. The Ghostface Y2K trend puts you in a dreamy, grainy early-2000s bedroom (satin bedding, a chunky phone, lamp light) while a masked figure watches from a dim doorway. It spread across TikTok, Instagram Reels and Shorts in September, with Gemini's Nano Banana model as the usual tool.

Classic costumes are still popular too: witches, vampires, Victorian ghosts and skeleton face paint. The difference now is that people want the result to look like *them*, not a stranger in a costume. Every prompt below is written with that in mind.

## Before you start: pick the right selfie

- **One face, clearly visible.** Front-facing or slightly angled, no sunglasses, no hand over your face.
- **Good, even light.** Daylight from a window is ideal. Dark, noisy party photos give the AI less to work with.
- **Not too close.** Head and shoulders, with some space around you, gives the AI room to add costumes and scenes.
- **Plain background is fine.** The AI will replace it anyway.

## The Ghostface Y2K trend prompts

### 1. The classic Y2K bedroom scene (best in Gemini)

```
Turn my selfie into a dreamy early-2000s style photo. Keep my face, skin tone and hairstyle exactly the same. I'm lying on shiny pink satin bedding holding a chunky cordless phone, with butterfly clips in my hair and posters on the wall. Warm lamp light in a dark room, grainy film look. Behind me, a tall figure in a black hooded robe and a white ghost mask stands in the dim hallway doorway, barely lit, watching.
```

[IMAGE: before/after, Prompt 1]

### 2. The late-night phone call (more cinematic)

```
Using my uploaded photo, create a cinematic Y2K-era scene of me sitting on the edge of my bed at night, holding a corded landline phone to my ear with a worried expression. Preserve my exact face, features and skin tone. Warm bedside lamp, cool blue moonlight from the window, posters and fairy lights, visible film grain. In the dark doorway behind me, a masked figure in a black hooded robe stands still, mostly in shadow.
```

### 3. Ghostface version for men

The original trend was very "cozy-girl", so here's a version with a different setting.

```
Turn this photo into a grainy 2000s-style photo of me on a couch in a dim living room, holding a game controller, with a CRT TV glowing in front of me. Keep my face, beard or stubble, hairstyle and skin tone exactly the same. Hoodie and jeans, pizza box on the table, blue TV glow on my face. Behind me, at the end of the dark hallway, a figure in a black hooded robe and white ghost mask is barely visible.
```

### 4. Couple or friends version

```
Recreate this photo of us as a cozy early-2000s sleepover snapshot. Preserve each person's face, age and skin tone separately; do not blend or swap anyone's features. Everyone on a bed with pastel bedding, popcorn, a flip phone and magazines, laughing. Harsh on-camera flash, grainy film, slightly washed-out colors. In the doorway behind us, a masked figure in a black hooded robe stands in the shadows.
```

## Classic costume selfie prompts

### 5. Victorian ghost (inspired by Google's own example)

Google suggested a Victorian ghost portrait in its Halloween tips. This version keeps more of your likeness.

```
Transform me into a translucent Victorian ghost. Keep my facial features recognizable. High-collared Victorian clothing, pale ghostly skin, slightly see-through body. Keep my existing pose and framing. Black-and-white wet-plate photograph with streaks, stains and soft edges.
```

### 6. Elegant gothic vampire (best in ChatGPT)

ChatGPT handles detailed wardrobe lists well, so this one is longer.

```
Using my uploaded selfie, create an elegant gothic vampire portrait. Preserve my exact face shape, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone and natural skin texture. Do not make me look like a different person.
Outfit: black velvet high-collar coat with a deep crimson silk lining, a vintage cameo brooch at the throat.
Details: pale complexion, subtle dark under-eye shadow, dark red lips, a faint glint of fangs only if my mouth is slightly open.
Setting: a candlelit gothic library, warm candlelight from the side, deep shadows.
Framing: chest-up portrait, 4:5, sharp focus on the eyes.
```

### 7. Friendly witch with a glowing hand

```
Turn me into a friendly witch. Keep my face, hairstyle and skin tone exactly the same. Tall black pointed hat, dark green velvet dress, a soft green glow coming from one raised hand. Background: a cozy cottage kitchen with hanging herbs, jars and a bubbling cauldron, warm firelight.
```

### 8. Skeleton face paint (makeup look, not a costume)

Good if you want a "real makeup" vibe instead of a full scene.

```
Add detailed skeleton face paint to my face in this photo. Keep my face shape, eyes, hair and expression exactly the same, only add the makeup. White base, black eye sockets, nose outline and stitched teeth drawn across my lips, fine cracks at the temples. Keep my real skin texture visible under the paint. Plain dark background, soft studio light.
```

### 9. Zombie, but still you

```
Turn me into a zombie for a Halloween photo while keeping me recognizable: same face shape, eyes, hairline and features. Pale greyish skin, dark circles, a few light scratches, torn and dirty clothes. Foggy abandoned street at night, flickering streetlight. Keep it spooky but not gory: no blood or wounds.
```

### 10. Pumpkin patch portrait (cozy, not scary)

For anyone who wants a Halloween post without horror.

```
Place me in a pumpkin patch at golden hour. Keep my face, hair and skin tone exactly as in the photo. Chunky cream knit sweater, sitting on a hay bale surrounded by pumpkins, warm low sunlight from behind, soft background blur, natural smile.
```

## Fix-up prompts

### 11. When the AI changes your face

Send this in the **same chat**, right after the result appears.

```
Keep the costume, background and lighting exactly as they are. Only fix my face so it matches the original uploaded photo: same eyes, nose, mouth, jawline and skin tone. Don't smooth my skin.
```

### 12. When the masked figure is missing or sitting on top of you

```
Keep me and the room exactly the same. Move the masked figure so it stands clearly behind me in the dark doorway, smaller and partly in shadow, not overlapping my face or body.
```

## Tips for each tool

### Gemini (Nano Banana 2)
- **Where to find it:** Open the Gemini app or gemini.google.com, choose *Create images* from the tools menu, attach your selfie, and paste the prompt.
- **Keep it focused.** Gemini works best with a few clear changes per message. If a prompt has a lot going on, do the costume first and the background in a follow-up.
- **Fix in the same chat.** "Only change X" follow-ups usually keep more of what you liked than starting a new chat.
- **Paid plans:** Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can choose *Redo with Pro* from the three-dot menu to rerun an image with Nano Banana Pro.

### ChatGPT (Images 2.5)
- **Detail works.** ChatGPT handles longer prompts with separate lines for outfit, makeup, setting and framing (see Prompt 6).
- **Comment on the problem spot.** Images 2.5 lets you place a comment on one part of the image, for example "make the mask figure smaller", so you can fix one area without redoing everything.
- **Build in steps.** OpenAI says Images 2.5 keeps earlier changes more consistent across several edits, so it's fine to add details one round at a time.

### Google Photos Remix
Last Halloween, Google added a Halloween option to the Remix feature in Google Photos. Check whether it's in your app this season. It's a quick, low-effort choice if you don't want to write prompts at all.

## Common mistakes

1. **Naming a famous character and getting refused.** Some tools block named film characters or very graphic horror. Describe the look instead ("black hooded robe, white ghost mask") and keep it non-gory.
2. **Cutting the "keep my face" line.** It's the sentence that stops the AI inventing a new person. Leave it in every time.
3. **Using a dark party photo.** Grain and blur in the original make the face harder to preserve. Start with a clear daytime selfie and add the darkness in the prompt.
4. **Saying "spooky" without saying why.** Name a light source (a candle, a TV glow, moonlight, a single lamp). That's what creates the mood.
5. **Too many changes in one go.** Costume, makeup, background, lighting and a second character all at once often means something gets dropped. Split it up.
6. **Waiting until Oct 31.** Free plans have usage limits, and lots of people are editing that week. Make your images early.

## FAQ

### How do I do the Halloween AI photo trend?
Upload a clear selfie to Gemini or ChatGPT, paste a Halloween prompt that describes the costume, setting and lighting, and include a line telling it to keep your face the same. Then fix small details with follow-up messages.

### What is the Ghostface AI trend?
It's a 2026 trend where you're placed in a grainy, dreamy early-2000s bedroom scene while a masked figure in a black hooded robe watches from a dark doorway behind you. Prompts 1–4 cover solo, men's, couple and friends versions.

### Why is my Ghostface AI prompt not working?
Usually the tool is refusing a named film character or a horror element, or the figure lands in the wrong place. Describe the figure generically instead of naming it, keep the scene non-gory, and use Prompt 12 to move the figure into the doorway.

### Which is better for Halloween selfies, Gemini or ChatGPT?
Both work. Gemini is usually strong at keeping your face, and it's quick for short prompts. ChatGPT is good with long, detailed costume descriptions. Try your favorite prompt in both and keep the better one.

### Are Halloween AI prompts free to use?
The prompts on this page are free to copy. Gemini and ChatGPT both offer image editing on free plans with usage limits, and paid plans allow more generations.

### Can I make a couple or group Halloween photo?
Yes. Use a photo where every face is clear, and tell the AI to preserve each person's face separately without blending features (see Prompt 4). Groups of two to five work best.
