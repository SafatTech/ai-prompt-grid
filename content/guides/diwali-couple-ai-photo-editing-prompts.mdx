# Diwali Couple AI Photo Prompts

A Diwali couple prompt should look like a photograph of two specific people, with lamps, color, and clothing added around them. It should not replace the couple with a stock pose, redraw their faces to match a jewelry ad, or cover every surface in unreadable text. The prompts here are for one photo that already shows both people.

You paste the prompt into ChatGPT or Gemini and upload that photo. AI Prompt Grid does not generate the picture. Treat these as drafts until they have been tested and the placeholders below are real images. For the moonlit fast a few days earlier, the paired couple guide is [Karwa Chauth AI photo prompts](/guides/karwa-chauth-ai-photo-editing-prompts).

## What this solves

Festival edits fail when the prompt is only the name of the holiday. The model then reaches for the most common poster: extra people, perfect studio skin, and English words baked into the background. A better prompt says who must stay recognizable, what they are wearing, where the diyas are, and what text is forbidden.

## Photo checklist

- One photo, both people, faces large enough to compare later.
- Traditional clothing you already own is easier to extend than a request for a specific designer drape the model has never seen on these bodies.
- Jewelry can be added, but name it as jewelry, not as a change to the face. Nose pins and earrings should not move the nose or the ear.
- Avoid a source photo shot through heavy color filters. The filter becomes skin.
- If a child is in the frame, count them. Festival prompts like to add cousins.

Standing near a window or a lamp is enough. You do not need a pre-lit mandir in the source photo.

## How to run it

1. Open a new ChatGPT or Gemini chat.
2. Upload the couple photo, then one prompt.
3. Generate a single image with style presets left off.
4. Check both faces against the upload, then the hands, then the drape of the clothing.
5. If a face moved, use the repair prompt with the original photo. Do not ask for "more festive" on top of a wrong face.

## What must stay the same

Preserve both identities, facial features, skin tones, ages, and recognizable appearance. Do not slim, smooth, or beautify either face, and do not blend them. Clothing, jewelry, and the room may change. The number of people may not. Do not add religious symbols the couple did not ask for, and do not paint text, mantras, or greetings into the image. Those come out misspelled and are better added later in a design tool if you need them.

## Living room with diyas

```prompt
Using the uploaded photo of this couple, create a Diwali evening portrait in their living room. Keep both faces, ages, skin tones, and features exactly as they are. Do not blend, slim, or smooth their faces, and do not add people. Dress them in coordinated festive Indian clothing that fits their bodies naturally: one in a deep maroon saree or kurta with gold edging, the other in a complementary silk kurta or sherwani. Warm diyas on a low table, a string of small warm lights in the background, soft lamp light on the faces, realistic skin. No readable text, no logos, no extra hands.
```

## Doorway with string lights

```prompt
Recreate the uploaded couple as a Diwali night portrait in a doorway. Preserve both identities and keep their faces recognizable and unretouched. Do not change how many people are present. Add marigold garlands that do not cover their faces, and warm fairy lights around the door. Clothing should be festive and specific to each person, described separately so their faces are not styled as one look. Natural standing pose close to the original, realistic photograph, no poster layout, no text, no watermarks.
```

## Seated portrait, quieter color

Use this when you want the clothes and a few lamps, not a full set rebuild.

```prompt
Edit the uploaded couple photo into a quiet Diwali portrait. Keep the same pose, the same faces, and the same ages. Do not beautify the skin. Replace casual clothes with festive wear in saffron, maroon, and gold, fitted naturally, and add two or three lit diyas on a surface already in the frame. Background stays the real room, only warmer. No additional relatives, no text, no floating sparkles across the faces.
```

## Repair prompt

```prompt
Start over from the original couple photo. Restore both real faces, skin tones, ages, and the original number of people. Keep festive clothing and warm Diwali lights only where they do not cover faces or hands. Remove any text, extra people, and smoothed skin.
```

## Common failures

- Both people get the same jaw and the same makeup. Describe each outfit in its own sentence and repeat that the faces stay distinct.
- English or Hindi text appears on a banner and is wrong. Forbid all text in the prompt.
- Hands fuse around a diya. Ask for natural hands and look at them before you post.
- Skin turns orange from the lights. Say realistic skin tone, warm light on the room, not a color cast on the face.
- The room fills with strangers. State the headcount.

## Before you share it

Festival photos travel further than a test crop. Confirm both people are comfortable with the clothing and the setting. These prompts are drafts until the example images exist and the lines have been run on more than one couple. Generation happens in ChatGPT, Gemini, or another editor you choose, not on AI Prompt Grid.
