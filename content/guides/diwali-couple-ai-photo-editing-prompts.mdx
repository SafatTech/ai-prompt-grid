# Diwali AI Photo Editing Prompts: Couple, Girl, Boy & Family Looks

**Quick answer:** Upload a clear photo to Gemini or ChatGPT where every face is visible. Paste one of the prompts below and keep the line about preserving faces and skin tone. If anything drifts, fix it with a short follow-up message. These prompts are written to **edit your real photo**, so the result looks like you, not a stranger in a lehenga. You'll find couple prompts (including one for when you only have separate photos), girl and boy portraits, family and baby's-first-Diwali looks, plus versions for Diwali in Pakistan and abroad.

## Why "editing" prompts work better than "generate" prompts

Some Diwali prompts describe a whole new scene ("a happy couple in a red saree in front of a temple…"). Paste those with your photo and the AI often treats your picture as loose inspiration. Faces change, skin tones shift, and you end up with a lovely couple who aren't quite you.

Every prompt here starts from your photo and says clearly what to change (clothes, setting, lighting) and what to keep (faces, skin tone, expressions, body shape). That one shift is the biggest difference between a result you'll post and one you'll delete.

## Before you start

- **Every face sharp and well lit.** No sunglasses or heavy shadows. Daylight is best, and you can add the evening glow in the prompt.
- **Faces not too small.** If you're far away in the photo, crop closer first.
- **Two to five people works best for groups.** Bigger groups usually need more fixing.
- **Only upload photos of people who agreed to it**, and take extra care with children's photos.

## Fix-up prompt

### 12. Repair faces or skin tone

Send this in the same chat if a face or skin tone has changed.

```
Keep the outfits, background and lighting exactly as they are. Only fix the faces so each person matches the original uploaded photo: same eyes, nose, lips, jawline and natural skin tone. Don't lighten or smooth anyone's skin.
```

## Tips for each tool

### Gemini (Nano Banana 2)
- **Find it:** Open the Gemini app or gemini.google.com, choose *Create images* from the tools menu, attach your photo, and paste the prompt.
- **Three or four changes at a time.** Ask for outfits and setting first, then lighting or fireworks in a follow-up.
- **Fix in the same chat.** "Only change X" follow-ups usually keep more of what you liked than starting fresh.
- **Paid plans:** Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can choose *Redo with Pro* from the three-dot menu to rerun an image with Nano Banana Pro, which helps with family groups.

### ChatGPT (Images 2.5)
- **Go detailed.** Separate lines for each person's outfit, jewellery and pose work well (see Prompts 3 and 6).
- **Comment on one spot.** Images 2.5 lets you place a comment on part of the image, such as "her dupatta should be red", to fix one detail without redoing the rest.
- **Build step by step.** OpenAI says Images 2.5 keeps earlier changes more consistent across several edits.

## Common mistakes

1. **Pasting a "generate" prompt with your photo.** If the prompt describes "a woman" and "a man" instead of "us", the AI may draw new people. Say "edit this photo of us" and "keep our faces".
2. **Not protecting skin tone.** Warm, golden festival light can make the AI lighten or darken skin. Ask for your *natural* skin tone and use Prompt 12 if it shifts.
3. **Too much gold.** Gold outfit, gold jewellery, gold light and gold background can turn everything into one shade. Pick one or two accent colors.
4. **Disrespectful religious imagery.** Keep temple and home-shrine settings simple and respectful. Avoid prompts that put your face on deities or edit sacred images.
5. **Unrealistic fire near kids.** Keep diyas well away from babies, and only show children with sparklers the way they'd safely hold them in real life. It looks more natural, and it's the right example to set.
6. **Leaving it to Diwali night.** Free plans have usage limits, so make your images a few days early.

## FAQ

### How do I make a Diwali couple photo in Gemini?
Open Gemini, choose *Create images*, upload a clear photo of you both, and paste a couple prompt such as Prompt 1. Keep the line telling it to preserve both faces and skin tones, then fix any small details with follow-up messages.

### How do I edit a Diwali photo without changing my face?
Start the prompt with "edit this photo", list only what should change (outfit, setting, lighting), and add "keep my face and skin tone exactly the same". If the face still drifts, use the fix-up prompt (Prompt 12) in the same chat.

### Can I make a Diwali couple photo from two separate photos?
Yes. Upload both photos in the same message, say which photo is which person, and ask for matching lighting so you look photographed together (Prompt 4).

### Is it safe to upload my baby's photo to an AI app?
That's a personal choice. Check the app's privacy settings first, and think about who will see the result before sharing. Gemini images carry Google's invisible SynthID watermark, and ChatGPT images include C2PA metadata, so they can be identified as AI-made.

### Which is better for Diwali photos, Gemini or ChatGPT?
Both work well. Gemini is often strong at keeping faces and handles short prompts quickly. ChatGPT is great for long, detailed outfit and jewellery descriptions. Try the same prompt in both and keep your favorite.

### Are these Diwali AI prompts free?
Yes, the prompts are free to copy. At the time of writing (October 2026), Gemini and ChatGPT both offer image editing on free plans with usage limits, and paid plans allow more.
