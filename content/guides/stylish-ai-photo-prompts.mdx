## Stylish AI Photo Prompts: How to Use Them

1. Upload one clear photo with the face large and evenly lit.
2. Copy one prompt and paste it with the photo.
3. Keep the face, skin-tone, age, and hairstyle instructions.
4. Change one detail at a time if you want a different outfit, crop, or backdrop.
