# How to Merge Two Photos in Gemini: Copy-Paste Prompts for Couples, Families & Friends

**Quick answer:** In Gemini, attach both photos in the **same message**, then paste a prompt that says which photo is which ("photo 1 is me, photo 2 is my husband"), where each person should stand, and that both faces must stay exactly the same. Ask for one shared light source so you look photographed together. Fix lighting, height or blended faces with short follow-ups in the same chat. Google says Nano Banana 2 keeps the resemblance of up to five people in one workflow, so merge larger groups in stages.

[IMAGE: hero. Two separate selfies on the left, one merged couple photo on the right, same faces, matching light.]

## What Gemini can do (and its limits)

- **Combine photos:** Google's Gemini help page says you can upload multiple images and ask Gemini to create a new image based on them.
- **Up to five people:** Google's Nano Banana 2 announcement says it maintains the resemblance of up to five characters and the fidelity of up to 14 objects in a single workflow. Google Cloud adds that this varies by product and surface. In practice: up to five people per merge is the safe target.
- **Not on Flash-Lite:** The help page says the Nano Banana 2 Lite option, used when the model is set to Flash-Lite, isn't designed for multiple image references or multiple edits. Switch to another model before merging.
- **Not pixel-perfect:** Gemini redraws everyone into a new image, so expect "clearly us", not a cut-and-paste.
- **Labeled as AI:** Google adds its invisible SynthID watermark to images made with its models.

## Step by step: merge two photos in Gemini

1. **Open Gemini** in the app or at gemini.google.com and check the model isn't set to Flash-Lite.
2. **Choose *Create images*** (or just start a chat).
3. **Attach both photos in one message.** Tap the plus or upload button and select both. Upload them in the order you'll describe them.
4. **Paste a merge prompt** from below and edit the names, outfits and setting.
5. **Send, then check faces first.** If a face, the lighting or someone's height is off, use a fix-up prompt (Prompts 10 to 13) in the same chat.
6. **Download.** Google says you can download at 1K resolution without a Google AI plan and 2K with one.

## Pick photos that merge well

- **Similar light:** both daylight, or both indoors. Mixed light is the most common reason a merge looks fake.
- **Similar angle and framing:** two front-facing, chest-up photos merge more easily than a close-up selfie plus a far-away full-length shot.
- **Clear, unfiltered faces:** no sunglasses, beauty filters or heavy shadows.
- **Say real heights.** The AI can't tell from two selfies that one of you is taller.
- **Ask first.** Only merge photos of people who agree to it. Don't create fake photos of people together who never met or wouldn't want it.

## Couple merges

### 1. Basic two-photo couple merge (Gemini)

```
I've uploaded two photos. Photo 1 is me (the woman), photo 2 is my husband. Create one natural photo of us standing side by side in a park at golden hour, his arm around my shoulder. Keep my face and skin tone exactly as in photo 1 and his exactly as in photo 2; don't blend our features. He is about a head taller than me. Use one warm light source from the left on both of us so we look photographed together.
```

[IMAGE: before/after, Prompt 1]

### 2. Detailed couple merge (best in ChatGPT)

```
Use image 1 and image 2 as identity references.
Image 1: the man. Image 2: the woman. Preserve each face exactly: face shape, eyes, nose, lips, hairline, facial hair, skin tone and natural skin texture. Do not mix features between them.
Scene: a café table by a window, both seated, smiling at the camera.
Clothing: keep what each person is wearing in their own photo.
Scale: realistic, matching sizes; his head slightly higher than hers.
Lighting: soft window light from the right on both faces, matching shadows and color temperature.
Framing: waist up, 4:5.
```

### 3. Two photos in one frame, side by side

When you want both original photos kept as they are, in a single framed design.

```
Place photo 1 and photo 2 side by side in one elegant frame, as a single couple keepsake. Don't change either photo or either face. Add a soft cream border, a thin gold line between the two photos and a gentle matching color grade.
```

### 4. Long-distance couple in a place you love

```
Photo 1 is me, photo 2 is my wife. Create one photo of us together on Karachi's Sea View beach at sunset, walking hand in hand toward the camera. Keep both faces and skin tones exactly as in our photos; don't blend features. Casual summer clothes, warm backlight, sea breeze in her dupatta, matching sunset light on both of us.
```

Swap the place for any city or spot that means something to you both.

## Family and friends merges

### 5. Add someone to a group photo

```
Photo 1 is our family photo. Photo 2 is my brother, who couldn't be there. Add him to photo 1, standing at the right end of the group. Keep everyone already in photo 1 exactly as they are. Keep my brother's face and skin tone exactly as in photo 2, and match his size, lighting and shadows to the rest of the group so he looks like he was there.
```

### 6. Family merge from three to five photos

```
I've uploaded four photos: photo 1 is my father, photo 2 is my mother, photo 3 is me, photo 4 is my younger sister. Create one family portrait in a living room decorated for Eid: parents seated on a sofa in the center, my sister and I standing behind them. Keep each person's face, age, height and skin tone exactly as in their own photo; treat each as a separate person. Formal festive clothes, soft warm light on everyone.
```

For families of more than five, merge up to five first, then add the rest in a follow-up (see Mistake 3).

### 7. Friends reunion from separate selfies

```
These five photos are five friends, numbered 1 to 5. Create one candid group photo of all five laughing at a rooftop dinner with fairy lights, standing in order 1 to 5 from left to right. Keep each friend's face, hairstyle, glasses, beard or hijab and skin tone exactly as in their own photo; don't mix features. Same evening light on everyone.
```

### 8. Grandparent with grandchild

```
Photo 1 is my grandmother, photo 2 is my daughter. Create one warm photo of them sitting together on a charpai in a sunny courtyard, my grandmother reading to her. Keep both faces, ages and skin tones exactly as in their photos. Soft morning light on both, natural scale (a small child next to an adult).
```

### 9. You and your pet

```
Photo 1 is me, photo 2 is my dog. Create one photo of us together on a living room sofa, the dog leaning against my shoulder. Keep my face and skin tone exactly as in photo 1 and my dog's breed, coat color, markings and eye color exactly as in photo 2. Realistic dog size next to an adult, same soft daylight on both.
```

## Fix-up prompts for merged photos

Send these in the **same chat**.

### 10. Fix mismatched lighting

```
The two people look lit differently. Keep both faces and the background exactly as they are, and relight the whole image with one consistent light source from the left: same brightness, warmth and shadow direction on both people.
```

### 11. Fix scale and height

```
The sizes look wrong. Keep both faces and the background the same, but make the scale realistic: he should be about a head taller than her, both standing at the same distance from the camera, feet on the same ground level.
```

### 12. Fix blended or swapped faces

```
The faces have been mixed. The man must match photo 1 exactly and the woman must match photo 2 exactly. Restore each face separately, keep their natural skin tones, and don't change anything else.
```

### 13. Fix a "pasted-on" look

```
Person 2 looks cut out and pasted in. Keep both faces the same, and blend person 2 naturally into the scene: soft edges around the hair, a matching shadow on the ground, the same color grade, grain and sharpness as the rest of the photo.
```

For more face fixes, see **[How to Keep Your Face the Same in Gemini](/guides/how-to-keep-face-same-in-gemini-ai)**.

## Tips for each tool

### Gemini (Nano Banana 2)
- **Both photos, one message.** Uploading them in separate messages makes it harder for Gemini to tell who is who.
- **Name each photo** by number and say who it is.
- **Five people or fewer per image.** Add more in stages.
- **Try Pro.** Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can use *Redo with Pro* from the three-dot menu.

### ChatGPT (Images 2.5)
- **Number your references.** OpenAI's prompting guide recommends identifying each input image by number and purpose.
- **Comment on one person.** Images 2.5 lets you place a comment on part of the image, such as "match this face to image 2".
- **Sketch the layout.** OpenAI's new Sketch feature lets you draw a rough layout, such as where each person stands, to guide the image.

## Common mistakes

1. **Uploading photos in different messages.** Attach them together.
2. **Not saying who is who.** "Photo 1 is…" prevents swapped faces.
3. **Merging too many people at once.** Above five, faces are more likely to drift. Merge in batches.
4. **Ignoring light.** One sunny photo plus one indoor photo needs the shared-light line (or Prompt 10).
5. **Leaving out heights.** Say who is taller, or use Prompt 11.
6. **Merging without consent.** Don't put people in photos they didn't agree to.

## FAQ

### How do I merge two photos in Gemini?
Attach both photos in one message, then paste a prompt that names each photo, describes the scene and says to keep both faces exactly the same (Prompt 1). Fix any problems with short follow-ups in the same chat.

### What is the best Gemini prompt to merge two photos of a couple?
Start with Prompt 1 for a natural scene or Prompt 3 to keep both photos unchanged side by side in one frame.

### How many photos can Gemini merge?
Google says Nano Banana 2 keeps the resemblance of up to five characters in a single workflow, and that this varies by product and surface. For bigger groups, merge in stages.

### Can I add a person to an existing group photo?
Yes. Upload the group photo and the person's photo together, say where they should stand, and ask to match their size and lighting (Prompt 5).

### Can ChatGPT merge two photos too?
Yes. Upload both images in one message and number them. Prompt 2 is written in the detailed style ChatGPT handles well.

### Why does my merged photo look fake?
Usually because the two photos had different light, angles or sharpness. Use Prompts 10 and 13, or start from better-matched photos.
