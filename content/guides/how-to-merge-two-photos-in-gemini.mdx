# How to Merge Two Photos in Gemini (Couple & Family Prompts)

**Quick answer:** How to merge two photos in Gemini: attach both photos in one message, then paste a prompt that names each photo and keeps both faces exact. A merge two photos AI prompt like the ones below also works in ChatGPT if you number the images. Use them for a couple, a family, friends, or a pet.

## Before you paste

1. Open Gemini and check the model is not set to Flash-Lite. That option is not built for several reference photos.
2. Attach both photos in the same message, in the order the prompt names: first photo, then second photo.
3. Ask for one shared light so the people look photographed together.
4. Check the faces first. If the light, the height, or a face is off, use a short follow-up in the same chat. The tips at the end are for that.

Similar light and a similar crop merge more easily than a close selfie plus a far full-length shot. Skip sunglasses and heavy beauty filters. Only merge photos of people who agree to it.

## If the merge looks wrong

Send these in the same chat. They are follow-ups, not new style pages.

### Fix mismatched lighting

```
The two people look lit differently. Keep both faces and the background exactly as they are, and relight the whole image with one consistent light source from the left: same brightness, warmth, and shadow direction on both people.
```

### Fix scale and height

```
The sizes look wrong. Keep both faces and the background the same, but make the scale realistic: one person about a head taller than the other, both at the same distance from the camera, feet on the same ground level.
```

### Fix blended or swapped faces

```
The faces have been mixed. The person from the first photo must match that photo exactly, and the person from the second photo must match that photo exactly. Restore each face separately, keep their natural skin tones, and do not change anything else.
```

### Fix a pasted-on look

How to blend two photos in Gemini when someone looks cut out: ask for matching edges, shadow, color, and grain.

```
The second person looks cut out and pasted in. Keep both faces the same, and blend that person naturally into the scene: soft edges around the hair, a matching shadow on the ground, and the same color grade, grain, and sharpness as the rest of the photo.
```

For more face fixes, see **[How to Keep Your Face the Same in Gemini](/guides/how-to-keep-face-same-in-gemini-ai)**.

## FAQ

### How do I merge two photos in Gemini?

Attach both photos in one message, then paste a prompt that names each photo, describes the scene, and says to keep both faces exactly the same. Start with the riverside prompt. Fix problems with a short follow-up in the same chat.

### What is a good Gemini prompt to combine two photos of a couple?

Use the riverside prompt for one shared scene, or the keepsake frame when you want to combine two photos in one frame without changing either original.

### How do I add someone to a group photo?

Upload the group photo first and the missing person's photo second. The add-a-person prompt places them at the right end and matches their size and light.

### Can ChatGPT merge two photos too?

Yes. Upload both images in one message and number them. The cafe window prompt is written as a longer list, which ChatGPT follows closely.

### How many people can one merge include?

Keep it to five people or fewer in one result. For a larger family, merge a smaller group first, then add someone in a follow-up.
