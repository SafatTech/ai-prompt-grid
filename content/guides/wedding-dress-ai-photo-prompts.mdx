# Wedding Dress AI Photo Prompts

**Quick answer:** A wedding dress AI prompt — also called a wedding AI photo editing prompt or marriage photo AI prompt — changes the outfit, jewellery, and backdrop while keeping the uploaded face. Use these free copy-paste wedding photo AI prompt templates for Indian bridal and Pakistani bridal looks: bridal lehenga, sherwani, dulhan and dulha outfits, and couple edits. Upload a clear photo, keep the identity line, and try a gemini bridal prompt or chatgpt wedding prompt the same way.

## How to Use a Wedding Dress AI Prompt

1. Upload one clear photo (two photos for couple styles).
2. Copy a free copy-paste wedding photo AI prompt template below and paste it with the photo.
3. Keep every face, skin-tone, age, and head-covering instruction.
4. Change one detail at a time if you want a different colour, jewellery, or backdrop.
