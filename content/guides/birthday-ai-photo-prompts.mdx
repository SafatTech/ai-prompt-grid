# Birthday AI Photo Prompts for Girls, Boys, Men & Kids

The looks below mix girls, boys, men, kids, friends, frames, and a two-photo collage so you can pick a vibe fast. Every prompt is free to copy and paste, and works in Gemini and ChatGPT.

## How to make a birthday photo with AI

1. Open Gemini and attach one clear photo (or two photos for the collage prompt).
2. Copy a prompt below and paste it in the same message.
3. Keep the face-preservation lines. Change only the clothes, pose, or backdrop if you customize.
4. Want a name on the card or poster? Add a short follow-up in the same chat asking to include only your name under the "Happy Birthday" text where the prompt already allows that phrase.

## Tips to keep your real face

- Keep the face-preservation lines in every prompt. Birthday scenes often invent a generic smile.
- Ask for your exact skin tone, hairstyle, glasses, beard, or head covering by name in a short follow-up if anything drifts.
- Prefer a sharp, front-facing photo with even light. Beauty filters and heavy occlusion make identity harder.
- For kids and babies, use a photo taken at their eye level with the face fully visible and no stickers.
