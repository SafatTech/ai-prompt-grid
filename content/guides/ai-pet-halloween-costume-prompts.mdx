# AI Pet Halloween Costume Prompts for Dogs, Cats & Owners (Gemini + ChatGPT)

**Quick answer:** Upload a clear photo of your dog or cat to Gemini or ChatGPT, paste one of the prompts below, and keep the line that tells the AI to preserve your pet's face, fur color and markings. If anything changes, send the fix-up prompt (Prompt 14) in the same chat. Best part: your pet never has to wear anything. Below are dog costumes, cat costumes, spooky-but-cute looks, pumpkin patch shots, and matching costumes for owners, couples and two-pet households.

[IMAGE: hero before/after. Left: an ordinary photo of a dog on a couch. Right: the same dog in a pumpkin costume on a porch lined with jack-o'-lanterns. Caption: "Same good boy, now in costume."]

## Why pet edits need a little extra care

AI editors are good at costumes, but pets are easy to "average out". A tabby can lose its stripes, a white blaze can vanish, and a mixed-breed dog can turn into a generic Labrador. Hats and hoods make it harder by covering the features that make your pet *yours*.

Every prompt here asks the AI to keep your pet's **face shape, fur color, markings, eye color and ear shape**. Costumes are kept small enough not to cover the face.

## Before you start: pick the right pet photo

- **Eyes visible, markings readable.** A sharp photo where you can see the face and any distinctive patches.
- **Good light.** Daylight near a window works best. Dark, blurry photos of a moving pet are hard to edit well.
- **Whole head in the frame**, ideally with some body showing so there's room for the costume.
- **For owner-and-pet shots,** use a photo where both your face and your pet's face are clear.

## Dog Halloween costume prompts

### 1. Pumpkin costume on the porch (best in Gemini)

The classic, short enough for Gemini to follow every detail.

```
Put my dog in a simple, well-fitted orange pumpkin costume with a little green stem hat. Keep my dog's face, breed, fur color, markings and eye color exactly the same. Sitting on a porch step surrounded by glowing jack-o'-lanterns at dusk, warm porch light, shallow depth of field.
```

[IMAGE: before/after, Prompt 1]

### 2. Hot dog costume

```
Dress my dog in a funny hot dog costume: a soft bun on each side with a mustard stripe along the top. Keep my dog's face, ears, fur color and markings exactly as in the photo, with the head fully visible. Standing on a lawn with fallen autumn leaves, bright afternoon light, playful expression.
```

### 3. Lion mane

Works for dogs and cats.

```
Give my pet a fluffy golden lion mane costume around the face, like a real pet costume, not a real lion. Keep my pet's own face, eyes, nose, fur color and markings clearly visible and unchanged inside the mane. Sitting in tall dry grass at golden hour, warm side light.
```

### 4. Pirate captain (best in ChatGPT)

ChatGPT handles a detailed costume list well.

```
Using the uploaded photo of my dog, create a Halloween pirate portrait. Preserve my dog's exact face shape, ear shape, eye color, fur color, coat texture and every marking. Do not change the breed or make the dog look generic.
Costume: a small black tricorn hat tilted to one side, a red bandana around the neck, a tiny gold-buttoned vest.
Setting: the wooden deck of an old ship at sunset, ropes and a lantern nearby.
Lighting: warm lantern glow mixed with orange sunset light.
Framing: chest-up pet portrait, 4:5, sharp focus on the eyes.
```

## Keep-my-pet's-markings fix

### 14. Restore markings, fur color and face

Send this in the **same chat** if your pet looks different.

```
Keep the costume, background and lighting exactly the same. Only fix my pet so it matches the original photo: same face shape, eye color, ear shape, fur color, coat length and every marking and patch in the same place. Don't make the breed more generic.
```

**Tip:** If one specific marking keeps disappearing, name it: *"Keep the white blaze down the middle of the nose and the brown patch over the left eye."*

## Tips for each tool

### Gemini (Nano Banana 2)
- **Find it:** In the Gemini app or gemini.google.com, choose *Create images*, attach your pet photo, and paste the prompt.
- **One change at a time works best for pets.** Add the costume first, then the background in a follow-up.
- **Fix in the same chat.** "Only fix the markings" usually beats starting over. Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can also try *Redo with Pro* from the three-dot menu.

### ChatGPT (Images 2.5)
- **Go detailed.** List the costume, setting, lighting and framing on separate lines (see Prompt 4).
- **Comment on one spot.** Images 2.5 lets you place a comment on part of the image, such as "the ear should be floppy, like the original", to fix just that area. OpenAI says it also keeps earlier changes more consistent across several edits.

## Common mistakes

1. **Costumes that cover the face.** Masks, full hoods and big hats hide the features the AI needs to keep. Keep costumes around the body and neck, or push hoods back.
2. **Leaving out the markings line.** Without it, the AI often "tidies up" your pet into a generic version of the breed.
3. **Using a blurry action shot.** A pet mid-zoomie gives the AI too little detail. Pick a still, sharp photo.
4. **Going gory.** Blood and wounds look grim on pets, and some tools will refuse. "Spooky but cute" travels much better.
5. **Mixing up two pets.** In two-pet edits, describe each one ("the black dog on the left, the orange cat on the right") so features don't swap.
6. **Waiting until Oct 31.** Free plans have usage limits, and Halloween week is busy. Make your images early.

## FAQ

### How do I put my dog in a Halloween costume with AI?
Upload a clear photo of your dog to Gemini or ChatGPT and paste a costume prompt such as Prompt 1. Keep the line that preserves your dog's face, fur color and markings, then fix small details with a follow-up.

### Will the AI change my pet's markings?
It can. Always include a line about keeping markings and fur color, and if something still changes, use Prompt 14 and name the specific marking.

### Can I make a matching Halloween costume photo with my pet?
Yes. Use a photo where both your face and your pet's face are clear, and ask the AI to keep both the same (Prompts 11 and 12). Couples work the same way.

### Can I do two pets in one photo?
Yes. Use a photo of both pets, or upload two photos in the same message, and describe each pet by position or color so their features don't mix (Prompt 13).

### Which is better for pet costumes, Gemini or ChatGPT?
Both work. Gemini is quick with short prompts; ChatGPT is great for detailed costume descriptions. Try your favorite prompt in both.

### Are these AI pet costume prompts free?
Yes, the prompts are free to copy. Gemini and ChatGPT both offer image editing on free plans with usage limits.
