# AI Pet Halloween Costume Prompts

A pet costume prompt has one job: the animal in the result should be the same animal you photographed. Breed, markings, eye color, and the shape of the face matter more than the hat. Models like to "improve" a pet into a generic pumpkin dog or a cartoon cat. The prompts below keep the photo realistic and the costume simple enough that the face still reads.

Copy a prompt into ChatGPT or Gemini with one clear pet photo. AI Prompt Grid does not generate images. For a person in a costume, use [Halloween AI prompts for selfies](/guides/halloween-ai-prompts-for-selfies). Do not mix the two in one prompt unless the person and the pet are already in the same photo.

## What this solves

Owners usually want a costume they could have put on the pet for a minute, not a movie character. The failures are a changed coat color, an extra ear, a muzzle covered by a mask, and a body that no longer matches the pet's size. Naming the markings and telling the model what not to cover fixes more of those than the word "cute."

If you want a drawn or toy version instead of a photographed costume, the catalog already has those looks: [Cute 3D Toon Pet](/styles/cute-3d-toon-pet), [Cozy Plushie Pet](/styles/cozy-plushie-pet), [Hand-Painted Watercolor Pet](/styles/hand-painted-watercolor-pet), and [Storybook Adventure Pet](/styles/storybook-adventure-pet). [Funny Everyday Pet](/styles/funny-everyday-pet) stays closer to a real snapshot with a small wardrobe joke.

## Photo checklist

- Photograph the pet awake, facing the camera, with both eyes visible.
- Fill the frame with the animal. A tiny pet in a huge room gives the model room to invent a different body.
- Shoot in ordinary indoor light. A dark photo becomes fur the model redraws.
- Note the markings in a few words: "white blaze," "orange tabby stripes," "black ears on a gray muzzle." You will paste those words into the prompt.
- One pet per photo is easier. Two pets need an explicit count and a description of each coat.

Do not use a photo where the pet is already in a costume if you want a different costume. The model treats the old outfit as part of the body.

## How to run it

1. Open a new chat in ChatGPT or Gemini.
2. Upload the pet photo first.
3. Paste one prompt, with your pet's real markings written into the identity line.
4. Generate one image.
5. Compare coat, eyes, and ear shape with the original before you judge the costume.

[How it works](/how-it-works) explains the copy step. [Explore](/explore) lists the pet styles if you want a tested recipe rather than a Halloween costume.

## What must stay the same

Preserve the pet's identity, breed traits, coat color, markings, eye color, and expression. Do not turn the pet into a human, a plush toy, or a different species. Do not cover the eyes or the whole muzzle. Collars and hats can sit on the body. The number of animals stays the same.

## Pumpkin bandana, still the same dog or cat

```prompt
Using the uploaded pet photo, add a simple Halloween costume without changing the animal. Preserve breed, coat color, markings, eye color, ear shape, and expression exactly. Do not humanize the face or cover the eyes. Add a small orange pumpkin bandana and a soft knit pumpkin hat that sits between the ears and leaves the face clear. The pet stays in the same pose on a wooden floor, with a couple of uncarved pumpkins in the background. Natural indoor light, realistic fur, no extra animals, no text, no cartoon style.
```

## Black-cat cape for a real cat

Replace the marking note with what your cat actually looks like.

```prompt
Dress the cat in the uploaded photo in a small black cape for Halloween. Keep this exact cat: same coat, same markings, same eye color, same face shape. Do not turn the cat into a person or a stylized character, and do not hide the face with a mask. The cape ties loosely at the chest and does not cover the paws. Background is a hallway with one paper bat on the wall. Soft indoor light, sharp realistic fur, no extra cats, no readable text.
```

## Person and pet already in one photo

Use this only when both are in the upload. A selfie guide cannot invent the person, and this prompt should not invent a second pet.

```prompt
Using the uploaded photo of one person and one pet, add light Halloween styling. Preserve the person's face, age, and skin tone, uncovered and recognizable, and preserve the pet's breed, markings, and expression. Do not add people or animals. Give the person a simple knit sweater and a small costume headband, and give the pet a pumpkin bandana that does not cover the eyes. Living-room background, warm lamp light, realistic photo, no logos, no text.
```

## Repair prompt

```prompt
Redo this from the original pet photo. Restore the real coat, markings, eye color, ear shape, and number of animals. Keep a simple Halloween costume only if it does not cover the face. No cartoon fur, no extra limbs, no new animals, no text.
```

## Common failures

- The coat gets cleaner and the markings disappear. Paste the markings into the prompt in plain words.
- A hat covers the eyes. Say the hat sits between the ears and the face stays clear.
- The pet becomes a stuffed animal. Say realistic fur and forbid cartoon style.
- An extra paw appears under the cape. Check the legs against the original pose.
- Two pets become one. State the count.
