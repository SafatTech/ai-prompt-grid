## Tips for saree AI photo editing

1. Start with one sharp waist-up or three-quarter photo of an adult woman — face fully visible, even light, no beauty filters.
2. Keep every face-preservation line. Change only wardrobe, pose, or backdrop if you customize.
3. Leave clothing preservation off so the saree can apply; turn it on only to keep what you already wore.
4. If sequins smear or skin lightens, send a short follow-up: exact skin tone, lighter jewellery, true fabric texture.
5. Stay non-bridal here. For lehenga, sherwani, and dulhan looks, open the [Wedding Dress guide](/guides/wedding-dress-ai-photo-prompts).

## FAQ: saree vs sari prompts, face keep, and outfit change

### What is the best saree AI prompt for a woman’s photo?

Pick a look that matches the fabric and scene you want — party sequin, black and gold, Banarasi, or mirror selfie — then paste the full prompt with one clear photo and keep the face-lock lines. An ai saree prompt for photo editing works best when the source face is sharp and unfiltered.

### Saree or sari — which spelling should I use in a prompt?

Either works for the model. **Saree** is the spelling most people search; **sari** is the same garment (the title of this guide). Use whichever you prefer in follow-ups; the style pages keep your face either way.

### How do I change into a party sequin or organza saree and keep my face?

Copy a party wear prompt above, attach your photo, and leave clothing preservation off. The same face-keep advice as [How to keep your face the same in Gemini](/guides/how-to-keep-face-same-in-gemini-ai) applies. These templates also work as a ChatGPT saree prompt when you paste the full text with your image.

### Can I get a black and gold or Banarasi saree look from a normal photo?

Yes. Use the black-and-gold flower-steps look or the black Banarasi vintage sofa look — everyday photos are fine. You do not need to already wear a saree.

### Do these sari AI photo prompts work for solo portraits only?

Yes. This library is for solo adult women. Couple saree edits are out of scope here; for bridal couple outfit swaps see [Wedding Dress AI Photo Prompts](/guides/wedding-dress-ai-photo-prompts), and for generic couple clothes changes see [Outfit Change AI Prompts](/guides/outfit-change-ai-prompts).

### How is this different from the wedding-dress and outfit-change guides?
