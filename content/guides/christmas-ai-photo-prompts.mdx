# Christmas AI Photo Prompts for Couples, Kids, Girls & Boys

**Quick answer:** To make a Christmas AI photo, attach one clear photo to your AI image editor (two photos for the couple looks), paste a Christmas AI photo prompt from this page, and keep the face-lock lines so your real face stays the same. The looks below mix couples, kids, babies, girls, boys, men, a whole family and even a pet, built on trees, ornaments, gifts and candy canes. Every prompt is free to copy and paste.

All the people and the dog in the example photos on this page are AI-generated, not real people or pets. Every "after" image is an AI edit of those example photos.

## Quick Answer: How to Make a Christmas AI Photo

1. Open your AI image editor and attach one clear, front-facing photo (or two photos for the couple prompts: person A first, person B second).
2. Copy a prompt below and paste it in the same message.
3. Keep the face-preservation lines. Change only the outfit, pose, or backdrop if you customize.
4. If the face drifts, send a short follow-up asking to restore the original face, hairstyle, and skin tone.

## Tips to Keep Your Real Face

- Keep the face-preservation lines in every prompt. Busy festive scenes often swap in a generic face or smile.
- Use a sharp, front-facing photo with even light. Beauty filters, sunglasses and heavy shadows make identity harder.
- For couple prompts, use similar light in both photos and keep faces large and sharp.
- If anything drifts, name your exact skin tone, hairstyle, glasses, beard, or head covering in a short follow-up.
- For snowy looks without the holiday theme, see [Winter AI Photo Prompts](/guides/winter-ai-photo-prompts).
