# Pakistani Wedding AI Photo Prompts for Couples: Mehndi, Baraat, Nikkah & Walima (Gemini + ChatGPT)

**Quick answer:** Upload a clear photo of the two of you to Gemini or ChatGPT. Paste one of the prompts below for the event you want (dholki, mehndi, nikkah, baraat or walima) and keep the line telling the AI to preserve both faces and skin tones. If anything drifts, send the fix-up prompt (Prompt 14) in the same chat. Every outfit here is written to be modest: full or three-quarter sleeves, long kameez, and dupattas over the head or shoulders. There's also a prompt for when you only have separate photos.

[IMAGE: hero before/after. Left: a casual couple photo. Right: the same couple on a floral baraat stage, bride in a red gharara with a dupatta over her head, groom in an ivory sherwani and kulla.]

## Why "edit this photo" prompts work better

Many wedding prompt lists describe "a Pakistani bride and groom in a palace". Paste that with your photo and the AI may draw two strangers. Every prompt here starts with "edit this photo of us", then says what should change (outfits, setting, light) and what must stay the same (faces, skin tones, expressions, body shape).

## Before you start

- **Both faces clear and well lit.** No sunglasses, no heavy shadows. Daylight is best.
- **Faces reasonably large.** Crop closer if you're small in the frame.
- **Ask before you upload.** Many families prefer bridal photos to stay private. Only upload photos of people who agreed to it, and think about where the result will be shared.

## Baraat prompts

### 1. Baraat stage portrait (best in Gemini)

```
Edit this photo of us into a Pakistani baraat portrait on a wedding stage. Keep both of our faces, skin tones and expressions exactly the same; do not blend or swap features. Her: deep red bridal gharara with a long, full-sleeved kameez and heavy gold zardozi, the dupatta draped over her head, a matha patti and a small nath. Him: ivory sherwani with a matching kulla and khussa. Seated on a floral stage with roses and marigolds, warm golden light.
```

### 2. Detailed baraat couple (best in ChatGPT)

ChatGPT handles a detailed, person-by-person description well.

```
Using the uploaded photo of us, create a Pakistani baraat couple portrait. Preserve both faces completely: same face shape, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone and natural skin texture for each person. Do not beautify, lighten or change either face.
Bride: maroon and gold bridal lehenga worn with a long kameez reaching below the knee, full sleeves, a heavily embroidered dupatta over her head, a jhoomar on one side, a matha patti, gold choker and jhumkas, mehndi on both hands.
Groom: off-white sherwani with subtle gold embroidery, a maroon shawl over one shoulder, a turban-style kulla, khussa.
Setting: a wedding hall with a white and red floral backdrop and chandeliers.
Lighting: soft, warm, flattering.
Framing: full length, 4:5, both faces sharp.
```

## Two photos and style twists

### 12. Combine two separate photos into one couple portrait (Gemini)

Useful when one of you is abroad, or you don't have a good photo together. Upload **both** photos in one message.

```
I've uploaded two photos: photo 1 is the bride, photo 2 is the groom. Create one Pakistani baraat portrait of them together on a floral stage. Keep the bride's face and skin tone exactly as in photo 1 and the groom's exactly as in photo 2; don't mix their features. Red bridal gharara with full sleeves and dupatta over her head for her, ivory sherwani and kulla for him, the same warm lighting on both so they look photographed together.
```

**Tip:** Photos with similar lighting and head angles blend most naturally.

## Keep-the-same-face fix

### 14. Repair faces or skin tone

Send this in the **same chat** if anyone's face or skin tone changed.

```
Keep the outfits, jewellery, background and lighting exactly as they are. Only fix the faces so each person matches the original uploaded photo: same eyes, nose, lips, jawline and natural skin tone. Don't lighten or smooth anyone's skin.
```

## Tips for each tool

### Gemini (Nano Banana 2)
- **Find it:** In the Gemini app or gemini.google.com, choose *Create images*, attach your photo, and paste the prompt.
- **Three or four changes at a time.** Outfits first, then setting and lighting in a follow-up.
- **Fix in the same chat.** "Only fix the dupatta" beats starting over. Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can also try *Redo with Pro* from the three-dot menu.

### ChatGPT (Images 2.5)
- **Go detailed.** Give each person their own outfit and jewellery line (see Prompt 2).
- **Comment on one spot.** Images 2.5 lets you place a comment on part of the image, such as "make the sleeves full length", to fix just that area. OpenAI says it also keeps earlier changes more consistent across several edits.

## Common mistakes

1. **Not stating modesty clearly.** AI tools sometimes default to sleeveless blouses or short cholis. Say "full sleeves", "long kameez" and "dupatta over her head" every time.
2. **Using a "generate" prompt.** If it says "a bride and groom" instead of "us", you may get strangers.
3. **Skipping the skin-tone line.** Warm stage lighting can push the AI to lighten skin. Ask for your natural skin tone.
4. **Covering the face.** A full sehra or a heavy ghoonghat hides the features the AI needs to keep. Keep faces visible.
5. **Mixing events.** Yellow mehndi outfits on a walima stage look odd. Pick one event per image.
6. **Sharing without asking.** Check with both families before posting bridal photos publicly.

## FAQ

### How do I make a Pakistani couple photo in Gemini?
Choose *Create images* in Gemini, upload a clear photo of you both, and paste a prompt such as Prompt 1. Keep the line about preserving both faces and skin tones, then fix details with short follow-ups.

### Can I make a wedding photo if we only have separate pictures?
Yes. Upload both photos in the same message, say which is the bride and which is the groom, and ask for matching lighting (Prompt 12).

### How do I keep the outfits modest?
Spell it out: full or three-quarter sleeves, a long kameez, and the dupatta over the head or shoulders. If the result still isn't right, reply "make the sleeves full length and add the dupatta over her head".

### Will the AI keep my face the same?
Usually, if your photo is clear and the prompt says "keep my face exactly the same". If it drifts, use the fix-up prompt (Prompt 14) in the same chat.

### Gemini or ChatGPT: which is better for wedding photos?
Both work. Gemini is often strong at keeping faces; ChatGPT handles long outfit and jewellery descriptions well. Try your favorite prompt in both.

### Are these prompts free?
Yes, the prompts are free to copy and paste. Gemini and ChatGPT both offer image editing on free plans with usage limits.
